Liberation Times → Google News: Action Plan

Prepared 2026-08-24 · liberationtimes.com (Squarespace) · Site reliability review, social-distribution lens
Reality check: there is no Google News application anymore. Google automatically considers publishers for the News tab and Top Stories based on what its crawler finds. Your content and traffic are already there — what's missing are the machine-readable trust signals its policies require. Think of them as health checks: several are currently failing.

P0Trust pages — this week, all native Squarespace pages

Google News content policy explicitly requires clear author, publication, and contact information. This is the #1 blocker.

  1. About page — who Liberation Times is, mission, coverage focus, since when.
  2. Author bio page for Christopher Sharp — credentials, photo, contact, X link. Point every byline here, not the current /?author=… archive (robots.txt blocks author= URLs, so Google cannot see the only author page you have).
  3. Editorial standards + corrections policy — how claims are verified, how anonymous sources are handled, how readers report errors.
  4. Ownership & funding — who owns the site; state it is reader-funded (Patreon/PayPal).
  5. Upgrade contact page — keep the email, add publication/entity name and a contact form.
  6. Link all of these in the footer so they appear on every page.

P0Google Search Console — 30 minutes

Verify the domain at search.google.com/search-console (Squarespace: Settings → Domains gives the DNS record). The Google News performance report in Search Console is the definitive signal inclusion has started. Check weekly.

P1Technical fixes — next 2 weeks

  • NewsArticle structured data: Squarespace outputs generic blog markup. Add NewsArticle JSON-LD (headline, author, datePublished, dateModified, publisher) via Settings → Advanced → Code Injection.
  • News sitemap: Squarespace can't generate one — use an external generator/service, submit in Search Console. Spec: articles from the last 2 days only, max 1,000 URLs, with publication name, language, date, title.
  • Title consistency: make each post's SEO title match its on-page headline.
  • Dates: show full dates with year; show an "Updated" date on revised stories.

P2Content & distribution — ongoing

  • Sourcing hygiene: UAP coverage gets extra scrutiny. Link primary documents (FOIA records, filings), name sources where possible, label analysis vs. news. Anonymous "sources tell Liberation Times" as sole support is the biggest content-quality risk.
  • Topic sections: add nav categories (Congress, Pentagon, FOIA, Interviews) — helps Google classify the site as a news publication.
  • Own the audience: X-only distribution is a single point of failure. Add a newsletter (Squarespace Email Campaigns or Substack); let Google News become the second leg.
